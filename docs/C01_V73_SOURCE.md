## Paper-grounded statements

The publicly accessible abstract/Highlights support the following method-level statements:

- FBG decomposes sensor signals into frequency bands, builds one subgraph per band, and connects the subgraphs into a final graph.
- OCF applies a notch filter to remove operational components.
- Band-pass filters produce frequency-specific subgraphs.
- The public abstract describes a sensitivity-aware readout (SR).
- EA embeds energy-related features into graph nodes.

The public material checked here is limited to the abstract, Highlights and accessible preview; the full article text was not available for this review.

## Readout terminology discrepancy

The author-lab graphical abstract visually appears to label both readout blocks **IR layer**. The public abstract describes a sensitivity-aware readout as **SR**. The accessible author-lab copy is sufficient to identify the visible label as appearing to read IR layer, but it does not resolve whether this is a figure-label variant, a typographic/OCR ambiguity, or a difference from the abstract terminology.

Therefore:
- source-visible redraws preserve the author-lab label IR layer;
- the public-abstract term SR is disclosed in a note;
- the discrepancy is explicitly unresolved;
- no final terminology correction is asserted without the publisher original or full text.

## Source boundary

Repository outputs are editable derivative redraws, not the publisher image. Sensor photographs may be replaced with vector silhouettes, and graph coordinates/inter-band links remain schematic rather than a recovered numerical adjacency matrix. The author-lab image supports visible layout and shape comparisons; it does not establish exact graph topology, measured waveforms, numerical frequency cutoffs, pixel identity, or domain approval. Projection/print-size and independent domain review remain separate acceptance gates.
