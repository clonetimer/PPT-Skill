# C01 publication profile — independent design contract

**Status:** design only; neither Stage A image nor editable journal figure has been accepted.

The Image-first PPT-Skill supports two different review contexts. They must not be conflated.

## Presentation profile (current V4 target)

- 16:9 1920×1080 composition with title, method panels and concise right-side Reading guide / Evidence boundary.
- Stable native-object PowerPoint reconstruction after human Stage-A approval.
- Assess projection-scale readability and clarity of the method route.
- Full citation may be placed in the footer.

## Publication profile (planned, not yet generated)

- Two subfigures, labeled **(a) Frequency-band graph construction** and **(b) Graph-level fault diagnosis**.
- Move long Reading guide/Evidence boundary prose and bibliographic details into the caption and manuscript text.
- Retain necessary within-band/inter-band legend and the distinction between schematic glyphs and recovered numerical topology.
- Include variable M/N notation, circular + fusion, EA branch and two readout paths.
- Preserve author-graphic IR labels and include a documented terminology review against the publisher SR text.
- Output PDF/SVG plus an editable source file, with final-sized print and font verification.
- Assess at the target journal's single-column and double-column widths; never infer publication readiness from 1920×1080 pixels alone.
- Caption must identify schematic and illustrative elements explicitly.

The publication profile is not a silent reflow of the presentation layout: it has a distinct FigureSpec/content lock and review gate. It does not bypass scientific source verification.
