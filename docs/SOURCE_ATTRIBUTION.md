## Evidence labels
- source-visible redraw: the published figure or user-provided source graphic was visually inspected and its visible structure redrawn.
- paper/code-grounded reconstruction: the diagram is supported by article text, equations, captions, or official code, but the original figure image was not directly inspected.
- domain adaptation: the figure is a new diagram adapted to a research or engineering domain rather than a reproduction of a particular source figure.

Do not upgrade an evidence label without new source inspection.
