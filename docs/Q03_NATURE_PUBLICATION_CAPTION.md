# Q03 Nature-size example caption

Petal length by Iris species in the [UCI Iris dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/53/iris), DOI 10.24432/C56C76 (CC BY 4.0). Each small point represents one flower (50 per species; 150 total). Large black points to the right of each group show sample means; whiskers are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals from 2,000 within-species resamples (seed 76; resampling unit: flower). Horizontal beeswarm offsets are display-only and have no numeric meaning. No hypothesis test or published model result is claimed.

The vector panel is a **183 × 100 mm main-figure demonstrator** prepared against Nature's [figure sizing and accepted formats](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/building-and-exporting-figure-panels/) and [text specifications](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/preparing-figures-our-specifications/) as checked on 2026-11-07. Nature lists 183 mm as a double-column width, 170 mm as the maximum main-figure height, and PDF as a preferred editable vector format; SVG and PowerPoint are acceptable main-figure formats under the stated conditions. These rules differ from Extended Data figure formats. This is not a submission or an assertion of editorial acceptance. Final production scaling, actual-size legibility and scientific interpretation still require independent review.
