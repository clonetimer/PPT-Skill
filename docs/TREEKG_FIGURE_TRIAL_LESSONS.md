# Tree-KG source-figure trial: visual fidelity and occlusion

The source is Niu et al., [Tree-KG](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.907.pdf), ACL 2025, Figure 2 and Sections 3.1–3.3. The editable trial and source-style anchors are in `dist/skill_tests/treekg/`.

The first drawing preserved the two phases and operator names but materially changed the figure grammar. It enlarged the graph nodes into six isolated demonstrations, drew a long tan route through all six numbered operators, and showed only one state of the explicit KG. The route crossed panel content and implied a fixed execution sequence. The paper instead presents a flexible operator set, a before/after tree on the left, and graph transformations within the operator field. Therefore correct labels and a passing semantic-edge checklist did not establish source fidelity.

The revision removes the invented inter-operator route, restores the left-side tree before and after relation extraction, and trims graph connectors to circular node boundaries. Its independent topology fixture now asserts only the source-supported phase handoff and initial-construction flow; the six operator names are required nodes rather than forced sequential edges. The general `audit_figure_occlusion.py` preflight checks whether lines pass through text or ellipse interiors. It supplements visual inspection and cannot establish pixel identity, final-size legibility or domain review.

Remaining difference: the slide uses fewer example nodes, omits the source's robot glyphs and detailed matching/score loops, and enlarges labels for projection. Those choices make this a presentation-scale explanatory redraw, not a close facsimile of the paper artwork. A task requiring publication-style replication should retain the denser operator transitions or split Figure 2 into more than one panel at readable size.

The follow-up `TK02_treekg_source_composition` responds to a request for higher fidelity. It uses the Figure 2 aspect ratio, English source terminology, the original asymmetry of upper and lower operator fields, paired before/after graph states, pink summary nodes, a dashed threshold enclosure, a central LLM icon, candidate graph clusters and score arrows. Native PowerPoint shapes carry the scientific marks. The book and robot pictograms are licensed third-party SVG assets with transparent PNG fallbacks; their original files and license records are kept under `dist/skill_tests/treekg/assets/`. This version is a **source-composition study**, with small source-like labels; TK01 remains the projection-oriented simplification. One should not silently use TK02 as a full-screen presentation figure without an actual viewing-distance check.
